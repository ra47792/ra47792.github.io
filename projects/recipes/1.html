Why its so important to our family

Bamya is one of our most known dish and we cook it mostly during the winter time. Bamya is a comfort food to us because it reminds us of our home, bringing back alot of good memories eating it with the rest of our family back home.

Ingredients

  • Okra: 1 lb fresh baby okra or frozen cut/whole okra (do not thaw frozen okra before cooking).
  • Aromatics & Veggies: 1 chopped yellow onion, 4 to 6 minced garlic cloves, and we use jalapeño or chili peppers to spice up our dish.
  • Tomatoes: 1 1/2 cups of crushed tomatoes or use tomato sauce to make ur dish taste smoother, plus tomato paste and fresh diced tomatoes.
  • Meat (Optional): We usually use 0.5 - 1 lb amount of beef stew meat or lamb meat.
  • Base: Extra virgin olive oil or vegetable oil, plus chicken broth/stock.
  • Spices: All spices HAVE TO BE GROUND, coriander, cumin, cinnamon, or Lebanese 7-spice, plus lemon juice or pomegranate molasses added at the end for a zang taste.

Step by step cooking guide

  • Sear the Meat (If using): Always Brown your beef or lamb until caramelized, then simmer for 10-15 minutes, until tender (or use a pressure cooker for 45 minutes and for more tender results!).
  • Sauté the Aromatics: In a large pot, heat olive oil over medium-high heat. Add the chopped onions, garlic, and chiles, cooking for 30 minutes, until soft and fragrant.
  • Simmer the Stew: Add in the tomato paste, crushed tomatoes, spices, cooked meat (if using), the okra (okra must be washed beforehand) and the broth/stock and stir it all. Bring to a boil, then cover partially, lower the heat, and simmer for 45 minutes until the okra and meat are tender.
  • Finish and Serve: Stir in fresh lemon juice or pomegranate molasses for a tangy finish.

    • Pro tip: If you dont want the okra to be slimey add the okra to a pan and sauté for 5 to 7 minutes, roasting or frying the okra beforehand also helps.

Serving size

For one person: 150-200g of cut up small meat, 100g of okra, 1 small onion, 1 tablespoon of tomato paste, 1 tomato, 1 clove of garlic, 1 chile, 1 tablespoon of olive oil, and one cup of chicken broth.

For two people: 250-380g of cut up small meat, 300g of okra, 2 small onions, 2 1/2 tablespoons of tomato paste, 2 tomatoes, 1 1/2 cloves of garlic, 2 chiles, 1 tablespoon of olive oil, and 2 cups of chicken broth.

For three people: 300-420g of cut up small meat, 380g of okra, 2 1/2 small onions, 3 tablespoons of tomato paste, 4 tomatoes, 2 cloves of garlic, 2 chiles, 1 1/2 tablespoons of olive oil, and 2 1/2 cups of chicken broth.

For four people: 400-500g of cut up small meat, 400g of okra, 3 small onions, 4 tablespoons of tomato paste, 4 1/2 tomatoes, 4 cloves of garlic, 3 chiles, 2 tablespoons of olive oil, and 4 cups of chicken broth.
